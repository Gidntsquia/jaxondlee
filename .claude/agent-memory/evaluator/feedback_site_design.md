On 2026-09-23 the user rejected a redesign that used Homemade Apple headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible body text, a new palette and flat bordered cards. His words: it "looks totally AI; the font changes are silly and not simple/personal at all." In round 2 he approved a plain version: system font, white or near-black background, 1px light borders, no offset shadows.

He rejected two squids, the GiSquid icon ("looks weird") and a custom SVG, and asked for the plain 🦑 emoji instead.

**Why:** he wants the site to feel hand-made and simple. Novelty fonts or custom drawings read to him as AI-generated or odd.
**How to apply:** when judging design work on this site, look for novelty fonts, custom art and heavy restyling, and point them out to the user. Plain choices win.
